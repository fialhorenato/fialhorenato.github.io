I’m Renato Fialho, a software engineer at Booking.com in Amsterdam.

I work with Java, Kotlin, Spring, PostgreSQL, Apache Kafka, and AWS, building reliable software for real-world systems. Before Booking.com, I worked on payment products at [Klarna](https://www.klarna.com), [Toro Investimentos](https://www.toroinvestimentos.com.br/), and [Hotmart](https://www.hotmart.com.br/).
